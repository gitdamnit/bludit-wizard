# Changelog

Bludit Wizard follows semantic versioning. The authoritative, detailed changelog
is maintained in [wizard/CHANGELOG.md](wizard/CHANGELOG.md).

## v1.1.0 — 2026-10-18

Stable release.

- Twelve guided tasks across Write & Create, Edit & Improve, SEO & Quality and
  Ask Wizard.
- Task-specific page selection, instructions and contextual primary actions.
- Full-width workspace with original/proposed review and explicit approval.
- Read-only SEO, internal-link and content-freshness audits.
- Configurable OpenAI-compatible providers, searchable model discovery and model
  testing; OpenRouter and LLM Gateway verified in live use.
- Reliable form dispatch, server-rendered page selection and action labels, safe
  degraded-JavaScript handling, and versioned assets.
- License: GPL-3.0-only.
